Interactive atlas surface study

Move over the surface, drag to turn it, or focus it and use the arrow keys. Home resets the view.

4,096 points

4,096 vertices sampled from the week-36 population-average white-matter surface of the dHCP fetal surface atlas (CC0).

The way points glow and part around your cursor is interface artwork. It is not neural activity, biological dynamics or development, and the display orientation is not anatomically validated.

Model Morpher

3D surface interpolation,5.5 s recording

Blends the distance fields of two 3D surfaces and extracts the shapes in between, without matching vertices. Here, a dentate gyrus becomes a whole mouse brain (Allen CCF meshes via BrainGlobe).

Geometric interpolation, not anatomical correspondence or development.

Fly feeding circuit

Whole-brain fly model,9 s replay

In a 127,400-neuron implementation of a published fly model, cutting Roundup’s outputs reduced spiking in motor neuron MN9 in all 15 paired comparisons. The replay shows simulated motor spikes on a 3D fly.

One motor neuron in a computational model. The replay’s movement is uncalibrated and illustrative, from a separate simulation archive.

MorphBrain

Patterning model,runs in the browser

Move or remove SHH and BMP sources and watch the regions the model assigns change. I implemented the reaction–diffusion components and compared signalling conditions.

Illustrative patterning; parameters are not calibrated, and the folding aim remains open.

When does EEG imagery become decodable?

G27, a g.tec BR41N.IO team project

Our team asked whether early calibration could choose between CSP and Riemannian decoders, and how decoding depends on decision time. I contributed evaluation pull requests and obtained and relayed the organisers’ scoring rules.

Unchanged team figure. Separate fits on shifted 1 s windows, scored on previously inspected later training trials; feedback onset assumed. Three participants, six sessions; not a held-out gain.

About

I study neuroscience at UCL and build tools for exploring brains and data. As an undergraduate research assistant with Professor Lorenzo Fabrizi in UCL’s Medawar Pain and Somatosensory Labs, I work on EEG source modelling: relating models of brain activity to signals measured at the scalp.

Also on the full work page: a synthetic study of separating stimulus and choice signals when errors are rare, and the fly input-timing comparison.

I’m interested in paid internships, scientific software work and research opportunities in brain modelling, computational neuroscience and machine learning.
