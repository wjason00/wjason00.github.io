Interactive atlas surface study

Move over the surface, drag to turn it, or focus it and use the arrow keys. Home resets the view. Drag sideways across the surface to turn it.

4,096 points

4,096 vertices sampled from the week-36 population-average white-matter surface of the dHCP fetal surface atlas (CC0).

The way points glow and part around your cursor is interface artwork. It is not neural activity, biological dynamics or development, and the display orientation is not anatomically validated.

    NeuroPersona

    Built by a team of four. I worked on the avatar’s facial expression and gaze.

    1. Recorded activity Motor cortex, one participant, from an archived recording
    2. Two separate decodes A word, and an attempted facial movement
    3. Confirm or reject Keep the movement, or play the word with a neutral face
    4. Synthetic avatar Says the word with the chosen face

    A replay of archived public recordings (Willett et al., 2023), not live decoding. The word and the smile come from separate trials composed for playback, and the presenter operates Confirm and Reject manually. The portrait is fictional, the smile is interpolated and the voice is a stand-in.

EEG / team BCI project

EEG
decoding

When does EEG imagery become decodable?

  1. Get ready, 0–2 s
  2. Imagery, 2–3.5 s
  3. Feedback may be on from 3.5 s (assumed)

The 1 s window 2.5–3.5 s ends where feedback is assumed to begin.The 1 s window 3.25–4.25 s overlaps the assumed feedback by 0.75 s.

Both scores are from later training trials we had already inspected, with a separate fit for each shifted 1 s window. Not a held-out gain. 3 people, 6 sessions.

A team BCI project. I contributed evaluation pull requests and obtained and relayed the organisers' scoring rules. The figure is the team's, unchanged.

Selector result

A selector choosing settings from the 60 calibration trials per session scored 75/120, below 82/120 for fixed CSP.

Neuron sources and scope

Download the labelled neuron figure

Exact saved Roundup (720575940623211725) and MN9 (720575940660219265) skeletons in shared source coordinates, with the existing 0.5 µm display simplification. Every saved edge is retained. Source axes are not verified anatomical directions; no registration to the fly body is claimed. This is morphology, not activity, synapses or a signal route.

I study neuroscience at UCL and build tools for exploring brains and data. As an undergraduate research assistant with Professor Lorenzo Fabrizi in UCL’s Medawar Pain and Somatosensory Labs, I work on EEG source modelling: relating models of brain activity to signals measured at the scalp.

Also on the full work page: a synthetic study of separating stimulus and choice signals when errors are rare, and the fly input-timing comparison.

I’m interested in paid internships, scientific software work and research opportunities in brain modelling, computational neuroscience and machine learning.
