01 Active research prototype

2026–present

MorphBrain

MorphBrain asks how an explicitly defined molecular condition changes a broad positional fate map, and where an honest developmental model must stop. World A solves deterministic SHH/BMP reaction–diffusion fields and passes them through a project-authored positional decoder.

  • Computed: two scalar concentration fields on a fixed coronal grid.
  • Readout: broad dorsal, intermediate and ventral positional identity.
  • Stopping rule: field residual at most 10−4 plus a five-step identity hold.
  • Separate data view: CC0 population-average dHCP surfaces, GW21–36.

The atlas is reference playback, not the molecular model developing. Solver iterations are numerical relaxation, not developmental time; unsettled runs are rejected at a hard cap. Decoder constants and the stopping rule are project-authored rather than fitted biological parameters. The weekly white-matter surfaces come from the dHCP fetal surface atlas (CC0).

02 Desktop prototype

January 2026

Model Morpher

Model Morpher asks whether anatomical meshes with different topology can transition through a shared implicit representation. The Python prototype interpolates signed-distance fields and reconstructs each intermediate surface with marching cubes.

  • Pipeline: mesh repair › SDF sampling › interpolation › isosurface extraction.
  • Compute: CPU and conditional PyTorch/CUDA paths.
  • Recorded example: Allen Mouse CCF structures selected through BrainGlobe.

03 In active development

January 2026–present

Morphliner

Morphliner tests whether implicit-surface transformation can become a reliable browser-native authoring workflow. It arranges meshes on a timeline, computes SDF-based transitions with GPU and CPU fallback paths, and exports animation.

  • Editor: timeline authoring, camera, lighting and materials.
  • Engine: WebGPU/WGSL with a tested fallback chain.
  • Output: browser-side still and animation export workflows.

The through-line

Scientific questions first; related systems stay separate.

  1. 01
    MorphBrain

    How can molecular patterning be explored without confusing a model, an authored decoder and observed atlas data?

  2. 02
    Model Morpher

    Which implicit representations let unlike anatomical surfaces transition without shared topology?

  3. 03
    Morphliner

    Can those transformation methods become inspectable, reliable tools for authoring and export in the browser?

MorphBrain does not currently reuse Model Morpher’s SDF/marching-cubes path. Its atlas viewer uses registered vertex interpolation, and its molecular intervention does not deform that atlas.
