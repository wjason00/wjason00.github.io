MorphBrain atlas reference

The GW21–36 playback uses weekly white-matter surfaces derived from the Developing Human Connectome Project spatio-temporal surface atlas of the fetal brain. The source atlas is a population-average reference released under the CC0 1.0 Public Domain Dedication; it is not subject MRI and is not the molecular model developing.

The cortical folding backdrop behind the home-page hero is a derived rendering of those same sixteen surfaces: each week is rendered once, offscreen, into a single greyscale sprite sheet (assets/atlas/atlas-fold.webp) that the stylesheet steps through as a mask. It is observation, not model output, and it shows the sixteen real atlas timepoints with nothing interpolated between them. Weeks are rescaled to physical proportion using the per-week radiusMmPerUnit in the series manifest, so the sequence shows growth as well as folding. The generator is committed at scripts/atlas-fold/; it runs offline and never at deploy time. Being a derivative of a CC0 dedication, the rendering carries no additional restriction.

Source: Karolis et al., Developing Human Connectome Project spatio-temporal surface atlas of the fetal brain.

Bundled software and fonts

The MorphBrain browser build includes React, three.js, React Three Fiber, Drei, Zustand and their transitive open-source dependencies. The geometry decoder is Google Draco, licensed under Apache-2.0.

Three typefaces are used site-wide and served from this domain: Source Sans 3 for body and interface text, Spectral for headings and the italic notes, and DM Mono for labels, figures and the footer. All three are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Package-level copyright notices and licence texts are preserved in the complete third-party notice. The shared licence texts are also available under the licences directory.

The portfolio shell

The page you are reading contacts no other host. There are no CDN resources, no analytics and no external font services, and the Content-Security-Policy declared in its <head> forbids them — fonts, styles, scripts and media may only be fetched from this domain.

Its own stylesheet, two small scripts and the self-hosted font files listed above are the entire runtime. The fonts are the shell's only third-party dependency, and their licence is reproduced in full alongside the others.
