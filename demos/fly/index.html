What are MN9 and MN6?

MN means motor neuron. MN9 innervates muscle 9, involved in rostrum (main mouthpart) protraction. MN6 innervates muscle 6, involved in labellar extension. They control different parts of the feeding apparatus. This experiment measures MN9 only; it does not demonstrate movement or feeding.

Motor anatomy: McKellar et al. (2020), Figures 7 and 9.

Methods, limits & sources

Thirty one-second saved simulations: three recurrent gains, five matched input seeds and two conditions. Gain scales all signed internal connection weights, including inhibition and sensory-neuron outgoing synapses. Cutting sets only Roundup’s outgoing weights to zero; its incoming connections remain. External input is unchanged. Accepted sensory input also matches across all six gain/condition cells for each seed in this archive.

The schematic includes a direct Roundup-to-MN9 source connection but omits the rest of the wiring. It is not an anatomical map or a claim that this one edge explains the full effect. Physical connections constrain the model; this demo does not search the full raw connectome.

All fifteen saved pairs have fewer MN9 spikes after the cut. Five seeds are not five animals. One neuron, output and input regime do not establish biological effects, general robustness, novelty or full paper replication. The shared-candidate design and an execution correction were post-failure amendments.
