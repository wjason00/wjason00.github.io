Five artificial templates · one fixed model · one 500 ms measurement window

      The full published model runs at gain 1 and a 0.1 ms step for 1.5 seconds, with a 500 ms periodic input schedule repeated three times. The 500 ms analysis window follows a 500 ms warmup.
