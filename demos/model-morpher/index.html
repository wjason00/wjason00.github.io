Source and limits

Allen Mouse CCF v1.2 via BrainGlobe, 25 µm (Allen Institute; Wang et al. 2020). Model Morpher at f14ee06: resolution 128, 30 frames per transition, target 15,000 faces; 30 of 30 meshes returned.

  • Sampling uses deterministic vertex indices, floor(i·(N−1)/2047), on each surface. It is not uniform by surface area.
  • Topology and vertex counts change between frames, so a sample index is not a corresponding point.
  • No physical scale: each input was centred and scaled independently, so native sizes and atlas coordinates are gone.
Full provenance
  • Inputs: Dentate gyrus (726) and root (997), Allen Mouse CCF v1.2, BrainGlobe, 25 µm voxels. Citation: Wang et al. 2020, doi:10.1016/j.cell.2020.04.007.
  • Geometry: 30 actual saved production surfaces. No new geometry, SDF or potential volume was computed for this page.
  • Display: one shared numerical camera basis and one isotropic display scale fitted once across all frames. Same initial view direction as the recording, but not a pixel-identical match to its poster.
  • Encoding: little-endian Int16 XYZ, 30 × 2,048 points, 368,640 bytes; maximum quantisation error about 1.5 × 10⁻⁵ display units.
  • The optional crossfade overlaps two adjacent saved point sets for about 160 ms and always ends on one saved frame. It is not an extra surface or a point trajectory. Preset jumps between non-adjacent frames switch directly.
  • Reuse: local prototype authorised by the author. Allen/BrainGlobe source terms and citation apply. No CC0 claim.
  • Records: sampling manifest (JSON) · recording capture record (JSON) · source repository.
